# Ocean Blue \- Product Brief

**Industry — Food &amp; Beverage / Logistics**

**Product — OceanBlue**

## 1\. Problem

**SME food warehouse managers struggle to know their real stock levels and predict what will run out or expire, leading to stockouts, overstock, wasted food, and unnecessary manual work\.**

## 2\. Who it's for

### Primary User — Warehouse / Inventory Manager

**Name:** Aung Min<br>**Age:** 32<br>**Job:** Inventory Manager at a small food distribution company<br>**Location:** Yangon, Myanmar

### How he handles inventory today

Aung manages finished food products such as milk, yogurt, juice, and packaged food\.

Today, he may use:

- Excel / spreadsheets
- Manual stock counting
- Paper records
- Memory and experience

He regularly checks stock levels and decides when products need to be reordered\.

### Main frustrations

> “I am not always sure how much stock I really have\.”

“I find out some products are running low too late\.”

“I don't know exactly when or how much I should reorder\.”

“I may miss products that are close to expiry\.”

## 3\. What our product does

### Main User Flow

1. **Check Inventory**<br>Aung opens OceanBlue and sees the current inventory, low\-stock items, and products that need attention\.
2. **Update Stock**<br>When products enter or leave the warehouse, he scans/selects the product and records the quantity\.
3. **AI Detects Risks**<br>OceanBlue analyzes inventory data and identifies possible stockouts, low stock, and expiry risks\.
4. **Get Recommendations**<br>The system tells Aung what needs attention and recommends what to reorder, when to reorder, and which expiring stock to use first\.
5. **Ask AI &amp; Take Action**<br>Aung can ask, **“What should I do today?”** and receive a simple prioritized list of actions\.

### Example

**🔴 Chicken Sausage**

40 units left → Estimated stockout in 2 days

**AI Recommendation:**<br>Reorder 100 units today\.

**🟣 Strawberry Yogurt**

40 units will expire in 2 days\.

**AI Recommendation:**<br>Prioritize this batch for dispatch\.

## 4\. What we are NOT building

For this Buildathon, we will **not** build:

- A full Warehouse Management System / ERP
- Supplier management
- Accounting and payment features
- Delivery and route management
- Purchase order automation
- Complex demand forecasting
- Advanced barcode infrastructure
- IoT / smart warehouse hardware
- Multi\-warehouse management

### Our MVP focus

**Track → Predict → Alert → Recommend**

We want to solve one inventory problem well instead of building a complete warehouse system\.
